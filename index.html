FAQ

Common questions.

Do lessons with an instructor count towards my logbook?

Yes. In NSW, each one-hour lesson with a licensed driving instructor is recorded as three hours in your learner logbook, for up to ten lessons (30 logbook hours).

How many lessons will I need?

It depends on how much driving you've already done. After your first lesson we'll give you an honest idea of what you need to work on before you're test-ready.

How much do lessons cost?

Call or message us for current prices and package deals. We're happy to talk through which option suits you.

Which areas do you cover?

We're based in Fairfield West and cover nearby suburbs across Western Sydney, including Fairfield, Liverpool, Blacktown and Parramatta. If you're unsure, give us a call.

How do I book my driving test?

Driving tests are booked through Service NSW. We can help you decide when you're ready to book.
