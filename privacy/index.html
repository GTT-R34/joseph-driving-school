Privacy

This page explains how Joseph Driving School handles the information you give us through this website.

What we collect

When you request a call back, we collect the details you enter: your name, phone number, suburb, driving experience, preferred car type, best time to call and any message you add.

How we use it

We use your details only to contact you about driving lessons. We don't sell or share your details with anyone else for marketing.

Advertising and analytics

This site may use Google Ads tags to measure when visitors from our ads call us or send an enquiry. These tags can use cookies. You can manage ad personalisation in your Google account settings at adssettings.google.com.

Your choices

To ask what details we hold about you, or to have them deleted, call us on 0412 489 478.
