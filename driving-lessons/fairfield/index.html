Learning to drive around Fairfield

Lessons start on quieter residential streets, then move to the roads you'll actually drive on: The Horsley Drive, Hamilton Road and the Cumberland Highway. We also practise around the busy Ware Street shopping area, where pedestrians, parking cars and buses all need watching at once.

Do lessons count towards my logbook?

Yes. In NSW, each one-hour lesson with a licensed driving instructor is recorded as three logbook hours, for up to ten lessons (30 hours).
